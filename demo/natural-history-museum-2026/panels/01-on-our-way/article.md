# On Our Way

> *"First picture, on our way."*
> — Graham

Friday, 29 May 2026. Graham in the white cap, Charlotte beside him, the upper deck of the bus or the train carrying them in. Behind them through the window: grand red brick and a stand of green trees, London doing its show-off thing on a warm spring evening.

The trip in had been a small saga. South Western Railway was on a "Special Service" — that euphemistic phrase the railways reach for when the day has come slightly off the rails — and the run into town had been dicey. So *on our way* carries a touch of relief alongside the anticipation: the museum is ahead, the journey has held together, and the evening is officially happening.

This is the scene-setter. Bones and beasts and the year's best wildlife are still half an hour away. For now: two people, a camera, and the city moving past.
