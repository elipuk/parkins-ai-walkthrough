# Fini

> *"Fini."*
> — Graham

Back in Hintze Hall on the way out. The grand staircase rising behind them, the pale stone of the central court catching the evening light, Darwin sitting up at the landing where the museum has always sat him — patient, bearded, slightly amused, a marble man who has heard all of this before. [Hope](../03-hope/) the blue whale still suspended overhead in her dive. Graham and Charlotte in front of all of it, grinning.

The whole evening has come back to where it started.

Three hours ago they were on a bus, Graham in the white cap, Charlotte beside him, the city sliding past the window after a small SWR saga to get into town. Then through the gates, under the [terracotta Waterhouse facade](../02-arrival/), into Hintze Hall and *Hope*, the [What's-On board](../04-first-choice/) with its dinosaurs and discos, the [Wildlife Photographer of the Year](../05-a-monks-life/) [exhibition](../06-flying-rodent/) where the world kept recalibrating — wavelengths above violet, animals the size of a fingernail, a sloth on a fence post, a bat that had finally cracked flight, Charlotte's [editorial correction](../17-breeding-event/) on the frog placard, an ibex you could smell before you could see — and out into [Deep Time](../20-out-of-the-dark/), the plesiosaur in blue, the [T. rex Charlotte squared up to and Graham tamed](../21-tamed-it/) in the end.

And now: Darwin, a staircase, two faces, one word.

*Fini.*

It is the right word. (And worth saying out loud: this was Charlotte's evening to put together — her tickets, her Lates slot, her museum on her terms. Graham got to be the one with the camera. The arc of the day, gate to staircase, has her shape on it.)

It is short, dry, content, and slightly French in the way a person who has had a very good day allows themselves to be. It is also — and this seems important — the kind of word you only get to use *after* the thing was good. If the evening had been a disappointment, the caption would be longer. *Fini* is the caption of a day that delivered.

The picture itself doesn't need an article. They are happy. They are home, more or less; in the building they came in for. The whale is overhead. Darwin is at the top of the steps. They are grinning.

That's the day.
